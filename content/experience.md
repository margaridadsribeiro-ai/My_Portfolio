# Experience

## MSc Artificial Intelligence for Engineering and Design, University of Bath (2024 to 2026)

My master's degree focused on applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to
engineering problems. Through academic projects, I worked with data processing, feature engineering,
signal processing and machine learning models, developing an understanding of how to turn raw data
into something that can be analysed and evaluated.

For my dissertation, *Dual Modality Pulmonary Imaging for Bedside Monitoring Using AI*, I explored
how amplitude and phase signals collected during breathing could be used to support pulmonary monitoring
Amplitude signals provided clearer breathing patterns, while phase signals were noisier. I investigated
signal-processing techniques to enhance phase information using amplitude-derived information, then compared
machine learning approaches to evaluate the results.

The project taught me to approach technical problems systematically: understand the data, establish a baseline,
test different approaches and evaluate whether an improvement is meaningful rather than assuming a more complex
method is automatically better.

## BEng Computer Systems Engineering, Brunel University London (2020 to 2024)

My undergraduate degree gave me a foundation in computer systems engineering and the relationship between software
and physical systems. It developed my problem-solving skills and introduced me to engineering approaches for
designing, analysing and testing technical solutions.

Together, my degrees have shaped my interest in applied AI and software engineering, particularly projects where
programming, data and engineering come together to solve practical problems.
